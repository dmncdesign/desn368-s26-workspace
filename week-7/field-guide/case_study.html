Case Study

Stage: 01

Design Decisions

A lot of my design inspiration came from menus in Sonic Adventure 2 itself and notably the Chao Garden. The Chao Garden is what I based my color palette on, and my sidebar and headings are based on the SA2 stage loading screens. I'm making something about one of my favorite games that also has an aesthetic I love. I wanted to make something that wouldn't be too hard to code, either, although a few things look slightly different just due to my coding knowledge limitations.

Stage: 02

Technical Challenges

I was honestly struggling with flexbox at first. Looking things up and playing around until something worked and then getting to celebrate is a great feeling though. It really was just a lot of trial and error. I actually found a tutorial pretty quick on how to do the sidebar triangles, which I thought would be a lot more difficult. Oh, and position absolute took me forever to actually get positioned right... just kept trying different lines of code until I started to get it then it became a lot easier.

Stage: 03

Responsive Strategy

I wanted to collapse things down into a single column and also move the sidebar to the top rather than use a hamburger menu. I also did manage to change the triangle pattern along with it! I also wanted to adjust the size of pictures and textboxes if needed.

Stage: 04

Learning Moments

I learned a ton about flexbox and positioning actually. As I mentioned I struggled a lot at first... but suddenly once you do more, it starts to make more sense. I learned how to do position relative and absolute correctly, learned how to make multiple columns and collapse in mobile with flexbox.. and probably more I'm forgetting.

Stage: 05

Future Improvements

I would adjust some of the images more. I also may or may not be tweaking things for this constantly, so I'm getting it pretty close where I want. I think the things I wanted that I didn't were either too much to chew or just took too much time when I could do something easier. I'd also like to adjust sizing and max/min sizes.
